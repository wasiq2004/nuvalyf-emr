# Doctor guide

You see patients, write notes, diagnose, prescribe, order tests and sign. Before you can sign, your registration number must be on file. Check **My account**; if it says **Not on file**, ask your Clinic Admin.

## Today

**Today** shows patients **Waiting for you**, those **Ready (triaged)**, the **Longest wait** and how many you have **Seen by you today**, plus:

- **Pharmacy questions:** the pharmacist's questions about your prescriptions. Type **Your answer** and click **Reply**.
- **With you now:** a consultation you have open. **Continue consultation**.
- **Your queue:** **Call next patient** takes the next in line (emergencies and triaged patients first), or **Call** a specific patient.
- **Unread results:** lab results for your patients. **Open consultation** to review, **Mark read** when done.

## The consultation

Calling a patient opens their consultation. Triage vitals and allergies are already there.

1. **Consultation notes:** chief complaint, history, examination, plan and advice, follow-up. Click **Save notes** as you go; nothing is final until you sign.
2. **Diagnosis:** search ICD-10 by code or words, pick from **Clinic favourites**, or add a **Custom code**. Mark the main one as **Primary**.
3. **Prescription:** type a brand or generic name in **Add a medicine**. NuvaLyf checks the patient's allergies and current prescription for interactions:
   - **No allergy or interaction found:** carry on.
   - **Allergy**, **Contraindicated** or **Major:** you must give a **Reason to prescribe despite the warning** and click **Prescribe anyway**.
   - **Moderate** and **Minor** are shown for your information.
   - Dose, frequency and duration fill in from the medicine's defaults or a preset chip. Adjust as needed and click **Add to prescription**.
4. **Lab tests** (if your clinic has the Lab module): choose a **Test**, add a **Note for the lab** and click **Order**. It shows **Awaiting result** until the lab enters it.
5. **Sign & finalise** and confirm. The consultation is now locked: notes, diagnoses, vitals and the prescription cannot be changed by anyone. The prescription goes to the pharmacy queue and the visit is ready for billing.

After signing:

- **Prescription PDF** gives a printable prescription on your clinic's letterhead.
- **Amendments:** to correct or add to a signed consultation, use **Add an amendment**. The original stays as it was; the amendment is shown with your name and the time.

### Private note

**Private note** is for your own working thoughts. Only you can see it. It is not part of the shared record or the prescription.

### Taking over another doctor's patient

Only the doctor who opened a consultation can edit it. If a colleague is unavailable, open their consultation and click **Take over**. This is recorded in the audit trail.

## Pharmacy questions

If the pharmacist has a question about a prescription (for example a stock issue or an unclear dose), it appears on your **Today** page. Reply there; the pharmacist sees your answer on the prescription.
