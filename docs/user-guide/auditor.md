# Auditor guide

You review who did what and when. You see every action in the clinic, including sign-ins, record views, changes and refused attempts, but never clinical content such as notes, diagnoses or medicines. Where an event involved clinical fields, the trail tells you how many were withheld rather than showing them.

The trail cannot be edited or deleted by anyone, including administrators.

You land in the compliance panel. Clinic Admins reach it from **Audit trail** in their menu.

## Audit trail

Filter by **From** and **To** dates, **Person**, **Patient record no.**, **Event**, **Outcome** and **Severity**, then click **Apply**. **Reset** clears the filters. **Older events** loads more.

Outcomes:

- **OK:** the action succeeded.
- **Denied:** someone tried something their role does not allow. These are marked **Critical**.
- **Error:** the action failed.

**Export CSV** downloads the filtered events for your records. Exports are themselves recorded in the trail.

## Record access

**Who opened this record?** Enter a patient record number and click **Look up**. **By person** shows each staff member who viewed or changed the record and how often; **Every access** lists each event. Use it when a patient asks who has seen their record, or to check for curiosity browsing.

## Summary

**Summary** shows the **Most active people**, the **Most frequent events** and **Refused access** for the period. Look out for:

- repeated refused access by one person,
- a staff member opening many records they did not treat,
- activity outside working hours,
- many failed sign-ins.

Raise anything unusual with the Clinic Admin or your compliance officer.
