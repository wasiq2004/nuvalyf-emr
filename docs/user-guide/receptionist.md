# Receptionist guide

You register patients, book and check them in, keep the queue moving and take payment. You do not see clinical notes.

## Today

**Today** is your home page. It shows how many patients are **Booked today**, **Still to arrive**, **In the clinic now** and **Seen today**, plus:

- **Urgent:** emergency patients whose record still needs details. Click **Complete record**.
- **To bill:** finished visits without an invoice. Click **Bill**.
- **Queue now:** who is waiting.
- **Still to arrive:** today's bookings not yet checked in. **Late** marks anyone past their time.

## Registering a patient

Always search before registering, so the same person does not get two records.

1. **Patients → Register a new patient**.
2. Under **1. Who is it?**, enter the first and last name, mobile, and date of birth (or age), then click **Search for existing patients**.
3. If **2. Existing records** shows a match marked **Likely the same person**, click **Use this record**.
4. Otherwise, under **3. Register**, add sex and any other details and click **Register patient**. If NuvaLyf found a likely match, it asks **Why is a new record needed?**. Give a short reason (for example "Different father's name, confirmed with patient").

Each patient gets a record number such as `P-000042`. You can search by name, mobile or record number from **Patients**.

## The patient page

From a patient's page you can **Edit details**, add them as a **Walk-in**, or **Book appointment**. It also shows their allergies, consultations (signed or open), invoices, appointments and visits.

**Messaging consent:** ask the patient whether they agree to WhatsApp appointment reminders and to clinic updates (broadcasts). Click **Patient agrees** or **Withdraw** for each. Every change is kept in **History**.

## Appointments

**Appointments** shows one day at a time. Use the arrows or date picker to change day, and the doctor filter to show one doctor.

- **Book appointment:** choose the patient (or **Register them first**), the doctor, service and date, then pick a time and click **Book**.
- **Check in** when the patient arrives. They join the queue.
- **Reschedule** (calendar icon): choose a new **Day** and time, then **Move to**.
- **No-show** (icon): marks a patient who did not come.
- **Cancel** (icon): enter a **Reason** and click **Cancel appointment**.

Doctors on leave cannot be booked that day.

## Walk-ins and the queue

**Queue → Add a walk-in**, or **Walk-in** on the patient's page: choose a **Doctor** (or **Any doctor**) and **Service**, then **Add to queue**.

The queue shows each patient's position, status and how long they have waited (amber after 20 minutes, red after 45). Statuses run **Waiting → In triage → Ready for doctor → With doctor → Completed**. Emergencies always come first.

- Use the up and down arrows to change the order when needed.
- If a patient leaves before being seen, click the leave icon and confirm **Mark as left**.

## Billing

**Billing** shows what was **Collected today**, what is **Outstanding**, and visits still **To bill today**. Filter with **Outstanding**, **Drafts**, **Today** or **All**.

1. Click **Bill** on Today (or **New invoice** on the patient's page). The visit's service and any lab tests ordered in the consultation are added automatically.
2. Add more lines: choose a **Service** (or **Other item…**), quantity and price, then **Add**.
3. For a discount, enter the **Discount** and a **Reason**, then **Apply**.
4. Click **Issue** to finalise. An issued invoice cannot be edited. A draft you no longer need can be removed with **Discard draft**.
5. **Take payment:** enter the **Amount**, how it was **Paid by** (Cash, UPI, Card, Other) and any reference, then **Record payment**. Part payments are fine; the invoice shows **Part paid** until it is settled.
6. **PDF** gives a printable invoice.

Clicking twice or losing connection never records a payment twice.

**Refund** and **Void** need a reason and are recorded in the audit trail.

## Broadcasts

If your role allows it and WhatsApp is set up, **Broadcasts** sends an approved message to many patients:

1. Enter a **Title (for your records)** and choose a **Template**.
2. Choose the audience: **Every patient** or **Patients seen in the last N days**.
3. Check the preview and counts. Patients without consent or a mobile number are left out automatically.
4. Click **Review and send**, then **Send now**.
